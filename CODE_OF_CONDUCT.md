<!-- ────────────  PLEDGE  ──────────── -->

<h2>
  <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide:sprout.svg?color=%2322c55e&width=22" align="center" />
  &nbsp;Our Pledge
</h2>

<p>
  We as members, maintainers, and contributors of the <b>WorksheetJS</b> community pledge to make participation in our project and our community a <b>harassment-free experience for everyone</b> — regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
</p>

<p>
  We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an <b>open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy</b> community.
</p>

<!-- ────────────  ENFORCEMENT RESPONSIBILITIES  ──────────── -->

<h2>
  <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide:shield.svg?color=%237C3AED&width=22" align="center" />
  &nbsp;Enforcement Responsibilities
</h2>

<p>
  Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior, and will take <b>appropriate, fair corrective action</b> in response to anything they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
</p>

<p>
  Maintainers have the right and responsibility to <b>remove, edit, or reject</b> comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct — and to communicate the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.
</p>

<br/>

<!-- ────────────  SCOPE  ──────────── -->

<h2>
  <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide:globe-2.svg?color=%237C3AED&width=22" align="center" />
  &nbsp;Scope
</h2>

<p>
  This Code of Conduct applies within <b>all community spaces</b> — GitHub Issues, Discussions, Releases, any chat (Discord / Slack), and official social accounts.
  It also applies whenever an individual is officially representing the community in public — using an official email, posting via an official social account, or acting as an appointed representative at any online or offline event.
</p>

<!-- ────────────  GUIDELINES  ──────────── -->

<h2>
  <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide:gavel.svg?color=%237C3AED&width=22" align="center" />
  &nbsp;Enforcement Guidelines
</h2>

<p>Maintainers follow these <b>Community Impact Guidelines</b> when determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct.</p>

<table width="100%">
  <tr>
    <td align="center" width="60">
      <img src="https://api.iconify.design/mdi:numeric-1-circle.svg?color=%2322c55e&width=44" />
    </td>
    <td>
      <b>Correction</b><br/>
      <sub><b>Community Impact:</b> Use of inappropriate language or other unprofessional / unwelcome behavior.</sub><br/>
      <sub><b>Consequence:</b> A private, written warning from maintainers — clarifying the nature of the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center">
      <img src="https://api.iconify.design/mdi:numeric-2-circle.svg?color=%23f59e0b&width=44" />
    </td>
    <td>
      <b>Warning</b><br/>
      <sub><b>Community Impact:</b> A violation through a single incident or series of actions.</sub><br/>
      <sub><b>Consequence:</b> A warning with consequences for continued behavior. <b>No interaction</b> with the people involved — including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct — for a specified period. This includes external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center">
      <img src="https://api.iconify.design/mdi:numeric-3-circle.svg?color=%23f97316&width=44" />
    </td>
    <td>
      <b>Temporary Ban</b><br/>
      <sub><b>Community Impact:</b> A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.</sub><br/>
      <sub><b>Consequence:</b> A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. <b>No public or private interaction</b> with the people involved — including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct — is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center">
      <img src="https://api.iconify.design/mdi:numeric-4-circle.svg?color=%23ef4444&width=44" />
    </td>
    <td>
      <b>Permanent Ban</b><br/>
      <sub><b>Community Impact:</b> A pattern of violation, sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, or aggression toward / disparagement of classes of individuals.</sub><br/>
      <sub><b>Consequence:</b> A <b>permanent ban</b> from any sort of public interaction within the community.</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
